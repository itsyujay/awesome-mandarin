# Awesome Mandarin [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Standard Mandarin Chinese, the official spoken language of China and Taiwan.

Dictionaries, apps, graded readers, HSK materials, and other resources for learning Mandarin. Entries are marked (free), (freemium), or (paid) when the pricing is clear. Resources for simplified and traditional characters are both included. This list is about Mandarin, not Cantonese or other Sinitic languages.

## Dictionaries

- [Pleco](https://www.pleco.com) - Offline Chinese dictionary app with handwriting input, example sentences, and paid add-ons such as OCR and extra dictionaries. (freemium)
- [MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary) - Web dictionary for simplified and traditional Chinese, with stroke order, stroke playback, and English definitions drawn from CC-CEDICT. (free)
- [Zdic (汉典)](https://zdic.net) - Chinese-Chinese dictionary with classical sources, radicals, calligraphy, and character decomposition. (free)
- [Moedict (萌典)](https://www.moedict.tw) - Open dictionary from Taiwan's Ministry of Education data, with definitions, radicals, and stroke animation, especially strong for traditional characters. (free)
- [Revised Mandarin Chinese Dictionary](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw) - Official revised Guoyu dictionary maintained by Taiwan's Ministry of Education. (free)
- [YellowBridge](https://www.yellowbridge.com) - Web dictionary with character etymology, radical breakdowns, and word-relationship charts. (free)
- [Outlier Dictionary](https://www.outlier-linguistics.com) - Expert character dictionary focused on form, phonetics, and historically accurate component explanations. (paid)
- [Hanping](https://hanpingchinese.com) - Android Chinese dictionary with handwriting input, audio, and optional professional dictionary packs. (freemium)
- [Trainchinese](https://www.trainchinese.com) - Dictionary and flashcard app with native-speaker audio and handwriting practice. (freemium)
- [Written Chinese Dictionary](https://dictionary.writtenchinese.com) - Searchable dictionary with pinyin, example sentences, stroke order, and character breakdowns. (freemium)
- [Dong Chinese](https://www.dong-chinese.com) - Character and word explorer with component trees, etymology, frequency, and example words. (freemium)
- [Purple Culture Dictionary](https://www.purpleculture.net/chinese-english-dictionary/) - Online Chinese-English dictionary with audio, sample sentences, and stroke-order animation. (free)

## Apps

- [HelloChinese](https://www.hellochinese.cc) - Beginner-friendly Mandarin course app with pinyin, handwriting, speech recognition, and an HSK-aligned path. (freemium)
- [ChineseSkill](https://www.chineseskill.com) - Game-like course app for Mandarin, with pinyin, characters, and a traditional-character option. (freemium)
- [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) - General language app with a short daily Mandarin course useful as a light supplement, not a complete study path. (freemium)
- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) - Spaced-repetition flashcard program widely used for Chinese vocabulary and sentence decks. (free on desktop and Android, paid on iOS)
- [AnkiWeb HSK decks](https://ankiweb.net/shared/decks?search=hsk) - Shared Anki decks searchable by HSK level; quality varies, so preview a deck before relying on it. (free)
- [Hack Chinese](https://www.hackchinese.com) - Browser-based SRS for Mandarin vocabulary, with HSK lists and sentence context. (paid)
- [HSK Lord](https://hsklord.com) - Spaced-repetition study lists for HSK 1-9 on the 2021 standard, with audio and optional practice tests. (freemium)
- [SuperChinese](https://www.superchinese.com) - HSK-oriented course app with lessons, speech scoring, and mock tests. (freemium)
- [LingoDeer](https://www.lingodeer.com) - Structured language course app with clear grammar notes and a full Mandarin track. (freemium)
- [HanziHero](https://hanzihero.com) - Character-learning app that teaches components, sounds, and stories in a systematic sequence. (freemium)
- [Clozemaster](https://www.clozemaster.com) - Sentence-gap practice in Mandarin, useful once you already have a starter vocabulary. (freemium)
- [Glossika](https://glossika.com) - Mass-sentence audio training for spoken Mandarin, including pronunciation drills. (paid)
- [Ninchanese](https://ninchanese.com) - Game-style Mandarin course covering vocabulary, characters, and HSK-style practice. (freemium)

## Characters and Stroke Order

- [Skritter](https://skritter.com) - Handwriting app that grades stroke order and uses spaced repetition for characters and words, including HSK 3.0 lists. (paid)
- [Hanzi Writer](https://hanziwriter.org) - Open-source JavaScript library and demo for stroke-order animation and writing quizzes. (free)
- [HanziStroke](https://www.hanzistroke.com) - Stroke-order dictionary with writing practice, worksheets, and HSK vocabulary review. (free)
- [StrokeOrder.com](https://www.strokeorder.com) - Stroke-order dictionary with animation and diagrams for simplified and traditional characters. (free)
- [Arch Chinese](https://www.archchinese.com) - Character dictionary, stroke-order practice, and printable worksheets aimed at students and teachers. (freemium)
- [Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi) - Open dataset of character graphics, stroke data, and dictionary fields used by many writing tools. (free)
- [Chinese Etymology](https://hanziyuan.net) - Historical character forms compiled by Richard Sears, from oracle-bone and bronze inscriptions to regular script. (free)
- [Zi.tools](https://zi.tools) - Character explorer for phonetic series, components, and related words, useful for seeing how characters group together. (free)
- [AnimCJK](https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK) - Open SVG animations of Chinese, Japanese, and Korean character stroke order. (free)
- [Hanzilla](https://hanzilla.app) - iOS app for handwriting practice of HSK and textbook vocabulary with spaced repetition, plus web stroke-order pages with a writing quiz for about 3,400 characters. (free)

- [Chinese Reading Practice](https://chinesereadingpractice.com) - Free annotated readings, from simple stories to selected news and essays. (free)
- [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com) - Import-and-read platform with a Mandarin library, tap definitions, and spaced vocabulary review. (freemium)

## Input Methods and Tools

- [Rime](https://rime.im) - Open-source input method engine for pinyin, shuangpin, Wubi, and other schemes, on Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile. (free)
- [Chewing (新酷音)](https://chewing.im) - Open-source Zhuyin (Bopomofo) input method widely used for traditional characters. (free)
- [Sogou Pinyin](https://shurufa.sogou.com/) - Widely used pinyin input method for Windows and mobile in China. (free)
- [Gboard](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.inputmethod.latin) - Google keyboard with pinyin, handwriting, and voice input for Chinese. (free)
- [iFlytek Input (讯飞输入法)](https://srf.xunfei.cn) - Mobile Chinese IME with strong voice input and handwriting. (free)
- [Flypy (小鹤双拼)](https://www.flypy.cc/) - Popular shuangpin layout and documentation, supported by many Chinese IMEs. (free)
- [Google Input Tools](https://www.google.com/inputtools/try/) - Browser-based pinyin and handwriting input without installing a system IME. (free)
- [OpenCC](https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC) - Open-source library and tools for conversion between simplified and traditional Chinese. (free)
- [繁化姬](https://zhconvert.org) - High-quality simplified/traditional conversion service with region-specific word choices. (free)
- [Chinese Converter](https://www.chineseconverter.com) - Web tools for pinyin conversion, simplified/traditional switching, and related text utilities. (free)

## Open Datasets and APIs

- [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict) - The standard open Chinese-English dictionary file used by Pleco, MDBG, Anki add-ons, and many other tools. (free)
- [HSK 3.0 Vocabulary](https://github.com/itsyujay/hsk3-vocabulary) - Open dataset of the HSK 3.0 (2025 syllabus) word lists, released under CC BY 4.0. (free)
- [HanziDrill MCP](https://hanzidrill.com/mcp) - Free, read-only HSK 3.0 data for MCP clients, also available at [mcp.hanzidrill.com](https://mcp.hanzidrill.com). (free)
- [complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary) - Machine-readable HSK 2.0 and 3.0 vocabulary lists in JSON. (free)
- [Hanzi Writer data](https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data) - Stroke-order data powering Hanzi Writer, derived from Make Me a Hanzi. (free)
- [Jieba](https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba) - Widely used open-source Chinese word-segmentation library. (free)
- [Jun Da character frequency](https://lingua.mtsu.edu/chinese-computing/statistics/) - Classical character-frequency lists compiled from large Chinese corpora. (free)
- [SUBTLEX-CH](https://www.ugent.be/pp/experimentele-psychologie/en/research/documents/subtlexch) - Word and character frequencies based on Chinese film and TV subtitles. (free)
- [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) - Python library of word frequencies across languages, including Chinese. (free)
- [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) - Open collection of example sentences, including a large Mandarin set, with translations and some audio. (free)
- [Common Voice (Chinese)](https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/zh-CN) - Open, crowdsourced speech dataset that includes Mandarin recordings. (free)
- [AISHELL-1](https://openslr.org/33/) - Open Mandarin speech corpus commonly used for ASR research, hosted on OpenSLR. (free)
- [Unihan Database](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr38/) - Unicode character database for CJK ideographs, including readings, radicals, and stroke counts. (free)
- [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org) - Open digital library of pre-modern Chinese texts, with search, dictionaries, and data access. (free)
- [BCC Corpus](https://bcc.blcu.edu.cn) - Large balanced Chinese corpus from Beijing Language and Culture University, searchable online. (free)
